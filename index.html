I am a Pharmacy student who is drawn to the science behind medicine: how a molecule becomes a treatment, and how careful research makes it safe for people.

I am still early in my journey and learning every semester. My goal is to grow into a pharmaceutical researcher who contributes, in time, to new medicines and better care.

Outside the classroom I enjoy speaking, debating and creative work. These keep me communicating clearly, leading with responsibility and seeing problems from more than one angle.
